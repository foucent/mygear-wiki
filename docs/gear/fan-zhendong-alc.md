## Overview

**Butterfly Fan Zhendong ALC** takes the outer-ALC formula that made the Viscaria a worldwide reference and reissues it under Fan Zhendong's name. In the words of our butterfly gallery, it is a **Viscaria-structure outer ALC** — the Arylate-Carbon on the outer side of the blade, firing early and easily.

Where it steps forward is presentation and a slightly modernised character aimed at the two-wing game Fan Zhendong plays: the blade responds fast from the first contact, and once you commit weight through the ball it delivers the crisp, high-output rebound that outer-fibre players look for. It is one of the flagship pairs our structure guide holds up as the model of *"high output at low effort"*.

## Feel & character

Because the fibre sits close to the surface, response starts at very low effort: blocks, flicks and backhand drives come out lively with short strokes. The blade is firm under the hand yet keeps enough flex to carry topspin on a full swing — an outer-ALC temperament, direct and immediate rather than dwell-heavy. See [outer vs inner fibre](../guide/outer-vs-inner-fiber.md) for how that compares with inner blades.
