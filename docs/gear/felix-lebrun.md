## The setup

He is Europe's rare penholder, and he plays the reverse-backhand attacking game that used to be a Chinese speciality — the backhand is a weapon, not a cover for the forehand. If you are weighing the penhold blades, our [Xu Xin Blue Label vs Tibhar Felix](../guide/xu-xin-blue-label-vs-tibhar-felix.md) comparison covers the construction arguments on both sides.

The blade is his signature **Félix Lebrun Hyper Carbon**, with his name and Tibhar's mark on the face and the Felix print on the red-and-blue handle. Before it he played Tibhar's Hybrid AC Inside.

On **both wings** he plays **Hybrid K3 Pro**, the newer version of the sheet he had before, Hybrid K3. The K3 family is Tibhar's hybrid — a grippy topsheet on a firm German-made sponge — and the Pro is the sheet on his racket now. Our [Rubbers price table](../rubbers.md) lists the Hybrid K3 at $49; the Pro is not on the table yet, so name it in the quote request.
