## The setup

His blade is the [Timo Boll ALC](timo-boll-alc.md) in a special order, and in penhold: Butterfly's own write-up on his grip, with his equipment as of February 2025, gives the Arylate-Carbon construction and the handle shape but no model number — the model comes from the shop, not from Butterfly's page. What he plays is not the flared market blade we list but his own penhold build of it. On **both wings** he plays [Dignics 09C](dignics-09c.md), the tackier face of Butterfly's Dignics line: the line's high-tension Spring Sponge X under a mildly tacky topsheet that holds the ball rather than skating over it. In our terms it sits in the mild-tack band — grip for short serve and the opening loop, but none of the slow, sticky character of a fully Chinese sheet.

The pairing is not the usual tacky-forehand, clean-backhand split. His game is the penholder reverse-backhand drive type, so the backhand is an attacking wing and gets the same sheet as the forehand; on the backhand he had [Dignics 05](dignics-05.md) before this, the clean non-tacky sheet of the same family. That is the pattern our [Dignics 09C page](dignics-09c.md) describes: the tacky face was once a forehand-only choice, and these days it turns up on the backhands of players who swing through from both sides.

He is the son of Qiu Jianxin, who has worked as Tomokazu Harimoto's private coach — the [Harimoto album](tomokazu-harimoto.md) is the other card on this page built around a ZYRE-03 forehand.
