## The setup

The blade is his signature [Fan Zhendong ALC](fan-zhendong-alc.md), Butterfly's outer-ALC reissue of the Viscaria structure — the Arylate-Carbon outside the core, so it fires early and rewards a compact, forward swing. He came to it from the [Viscaria](viscaria.md) itself, and before that the Infinity VPS V.

On the **forehand** he plays **DHS Hurricane 3 National** in the blue sponge, the national-team sheet: the tacky Hurricane 3 topsheet with the hard blue sponge behind it, the same family our [Rubbers price table](../rubbers.md) carries as Neo Hurricane 3 National. The surface holds the ball long enough to load a serve or a short push without much forward motion, and pays that back on a full swing. What the sponge colour changes is set out in our [blue vs orange sponge](../guide/hurricane-blue-vs-orange-sponge.md) guide.

On the **backhand** he plays [Dignics 09C](dignics-09c.md), Butterfly's mildly tacky hybrid: the one sheet that bridges the tacky forehand he grew up with and the clean, fast European-style backhand. Before it he had [Tenergy 05](tenergy-05.md) on that wing.

The pairing is the route the Chinese team has largely settled on at the top: the tacky national forehand for the first ball and the biggest swing, a hybrid on the backhand for the counter and the block. Both rubbers are on the [Rubbers price table](../rubbers.md); the blade is on the [Blades price table](../blades.md).
