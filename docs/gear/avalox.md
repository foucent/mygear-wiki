# Avalox Table Tennis Blades

**Avalox** is a classic blade brand founded in **1987** by partners from Sweden, Japan, and Malaysia. Its **Swedish-made** blades — built with the traditional method where adhesive slowly seeps into the wood under controlled temperature and humidity — helped champions like **Kong Linghui**, **Wang Nan**, and **Wang Tao** win world titles in the 90s.

---

## P500 — The Legend

**Avalox P500** — a classic 5-ply construction (**Koto** top layer, **Tanne** middle, **Ayous** center, about **5.7 mm** thick). The brown handle and red "cat's eye" cosmetics were once near-standard in many professional environments.
