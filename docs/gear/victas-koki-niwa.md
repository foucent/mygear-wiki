## Overview

**Victas Koki Niwa** is the signature blade of Koki Niwa, a player celebrated for touch, variation and almost unnatural feel for the ball. True to that identity, the blade is not about dominating with brute speed — it is built to make every kind of spin easy to produce and every placement easy to reach.

Its character is balanced and flexible: the blade bends noticeably under heavy loops, which gives generous dwell for spin, while staying controlled enough for the short game and quick changes of direction that Niwa-style play depends on.

## Feel & character

The signature quality here is *feel*. You can play soft, spin-heavy pushes and drop shots, then open up into full-blooded loops without the board changing character on you. Because Victas specifies this model in different builds over time, we always confirm the current plies, fibre and weight with the supplier before quoting your exact unit — if you have a preference, tell us and we will match it.
