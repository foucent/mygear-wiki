## The setup

The blade in the photo is **XIOM's Hugo Hyper Axylium**, with his name and XIOM's mark on the face and "3D fiber-textured tech, Korea made" printed on the blade itself. A note on our own pages: the [Joola Hugo ARY-C](../guide/joola-hugo-ary-c.md) guide still describes the ARY-C as his racket, and the [blades table](../blades.md) carries both Joola Hugo blades as well as the Xiom Hugo Supreme ALXi. The profile lists the Hyper Axylium, and that is what the racket photo shows, so treat the Joola blade in the guide as an earlier chapter.

On the **forehand** he plays **Joola Trinity Hugo Calderano Charged**, and on the **backhand** **Joola Trinity Hugo Calderano Dynamic** — the two signature sheets Joola built around him. Both are on our [Rubbers price table](../rubbers.md) at $62 a sheet in 2.0 mm or Max, red or black. Before them the forehand went through Target Pro GT-X51 and Jekyll & Hyde C57.5, and the backhand through Jekyll & Hyde C52.5: the older grippy line that the Trinity sheet replaced.

The shirt is **Polo Tempo**, the shop's competition polo in navy and in black.
