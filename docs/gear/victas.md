# Victas Table Tennis Blades & Rubbers

<p class="mg-prod-lead">Victas is the Japanese table tennis brand of the company formerly called Yamato Takkyu, which has made table tennis equipment since 1931 and took the Victas name in 2017. TSP was folded into it in 2021, and the current range runs from the Koki Niwa and Koji Matsushita signature blades to the V &gt; and Ventus rubbers.</p>
