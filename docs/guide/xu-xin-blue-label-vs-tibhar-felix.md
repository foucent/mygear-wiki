<div class="mg-compare-h">💡 Key conclusions</div>

<ul class="mg-check-list" markdown="0">

  <li><strong>Face wood sets the feel</strong> — Koto = crisp &amp; explosive; Limba = soft, grips the ball.</li>
  <li>Blue Label has deep bottom power (Ayous core + Stiga woodcraft); Felix is quick for an inner and fast to re-attack, but lacks depth.</li>
  <li>Felix is easier to play — stable and continuous. Blue Label has higher single-stroke threat but a higher skill threshold.</li>
  <li>Blue Label pairs best with Chinese tacky rubbers (<strong>Hurricane 3</strong>, <strong>Skyline 2</strong>); Felix pairs best with non-tacky or micro-tacky.</li>
  <li>Pick the <strong>Blue Label</strong> for aggressive first-three-shot play; pick the <strong>Felix</strong> for continuity and control.</li>

</ul>
