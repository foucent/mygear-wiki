    Hurricane 2 (Yan Sen)

    Hurricane 2 (狂飙2) — the personal blade of Olympic champion Yan Sen (闫森). Yan Sen (b. 16 August 1975, Xuzhou, Jiangsu) is a former Chinese national team player, currently Vice President of the China Table Tennis College and a coach with the Chinese national team. One of the most celebrated doubles specialists in Chinese table tennis history, he is known for his left-handed penhold reverse-rubber style combining loop drives with fast attack, tricky serve placement, and a deadly first-three-balls game. Yan Sen joined the national team in 1994 and from 1996 partnered Wang Liqin in doubles, forming the feared “Yan–Wang combination.” The pair took men’s doubles gold at the 2000 Sydney Olympics and repeated as men’s doubles world champions in 2001 and 2003, establishing China’s absolute dominance in the doubles events. In 2004 he was sidelined by a serious car accident that broke his right arm and he unfortunately missed the Athens Olympics. After retiring in 2006 he turned to coaching, guiding first the national women’s second team and then the first team, developing world champions including Guo Yan and Wen Jia. As doubles returned to the Olympic programme in 2024–2025, Yan Sen rejoined the national team’s coaching staff, focused on helping China break through in doubles.
